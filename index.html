Hanoi Window · 하노이의 창

A live window over Hanoi, Vietnam — 4K camera with a lofi, guitar, jazz and pop playlist that follows the time of day and the weather.

Live daily 05:00–18:00 (Hanoi time) on YouTube.

HanoiLive is the private tool that runs this stream: it schedules the daily YouTube live broadcast, fills in its title and description, and greets and answers live chat.
